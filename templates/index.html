    Top 4 Employment Scam Types
    1. The Fake Check / Equipment Purchase Scam

    The "employer" sends a counterfeit cashier check for $2,000–$5,000 to purchase home office equipment from their designated vendor. When the victim deposits the check and wires funds to the fake vendor, the bank bounces the counterfeit check, leaving the victim in debt.

    2. Telegram / WhatsApp Text-Only Interviews

    Advertising unrealistic salaries ($40–$80/hour) for entry-level data entry. The "hiring manager" conducts the interview strictly over chat apps (Telegram, WhatsApp) and never on corporate video calls.

    3. Crypto / Wire Transfer Mule

    Jobs advertised as "Payment Processing Assistant" or "Crypto Account Manager" asking you to receive payments into your personal account and forward them via Bitcoin, Western Union, or Cash App.

    4. Package Reshipping / Forwarding

    Victims receive packages ordered with stolen credit cards and reship them internationally, unknowingly participating in mail fraud.

    Job Seeker Safety Checklist
    • Cross-check the Company Domain: Official emails should end in @company.com, not @gmail.com or @outlook.com.
    • Verify on the Official Careers Page: Search the company website directly to confirm the job listing exists.
    • Never Pay Upfront Fees: Legitimate companies never charge applicants for background checks, laptops, or onboarding kits.
    • Demand a Real Video Interview: Legitimate employers use Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet with their cameras on.
    If you have encountered a scam, report it to the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and the FBI IC3 at ic3.gov.
